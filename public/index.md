# HypeCheck

HypeCheck is an open-source AI startup idea validator by NullHype AI.

It helps builders stress-test startup ideas before spending nights and weekends building the wrong thing.

## What HypeCheck Does

Describe a startup idea in plain language. HypeCheck returns a structured validation report with:

- Hype Score
- Competitor landscape
- Market sizing
- Execution risks
- Monetization viability
- Demand signals
- Build-or-don't-build verdict
- Public shareable report link

## Who It Is For

HypeCheck is for indie hackers, developers, product builders, founders, and small teams who want signal before committing to a build sprint.

HypeCheck is open source. The codebase includes a practical AI SaaS stack with Next.js, Supabase, Claude, Dodo Payments, public report sharing, report history, and legal pages.
